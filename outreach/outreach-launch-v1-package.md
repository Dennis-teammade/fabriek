# Outreach Launch v1 Package (Belgische KMO)

## 1. ICP Definition

### Primary ICP (highest priority)
- Company type: Belgische B2B dienstverlenende KMO
- Size: 5-50 medewerkers
- Revenue proxy: 500k-10M EUR annual turnover
- Digital maturity: uses CRM, email marketing, or at least structured client follow-up
- Decision maker: owner, operations lead, or marketing lead

### Priority Segments
1. Marketing & web agencies
2. B2B lokale dienstverleners (IT, finance, consultancy)
3. Opleiders/coaches met recurring offers

### Pain Signals (must-have)
- Leads are followed up inconsistently
- Team repeats manual work in sales/content/admin
- No clear automation layer between lead capture, opvolging, and reporting

### Exclusion Criteria
- B2C-only low-ticket e-commerce
- Solopreneurs without client acquisition budget
- Enterprises with long procurement cycles

### Qualification Rules
- Tier A: segment fit + clear pain signal + reachable decision maker
- Tier B: segment fit + probable pain, weaker urgency
- Tier C: weak fit or unclear owner; only include if list volume needed

## 2. 50-Target Sourcing Method

## Outcome
Build 50 qualified targets in 5 working days with consistent scoring.

## Data Fields (minimum per target)
- Company name
- Website
- LinkedIn company URL
- Contact full name
- Role/function
- Contact LinkedIn URL
- Email (if available)
- Segment
- Trigger (why now)
- Fit score (1-5)
- Personalization note (1 sentence)
- Status (`new`, `queued`, `contacted`, `replied`, `meeting`)

## Scoring Formula (1-5)
- Segment fit (0-2)
- Pain signal strength (0-2)
- Reachability of decision maker (0-1)

Score guidance:
- 5 = immediate outreach batch 1
- 4 = outreach batch 2
- <=3 = backlog

## Day-by-Day Workflow

### Day 1: Build seed list (15)
- Source channels:
  - LinkedIn search: "Belgie" + role + sector keyword
  - Google maps/sector directories for local B2B services
  - Existing network and partner referrals
- Add only Tier A/B leads.

### Day 2: Expand by lookalikes (10)
- For each strong seed, find similar companies:
  - LinkedIn "People also viewed"
  - Competitor client pages/case studies
  - Sector associations/member lists

### Day 3: Decision-maker enrichment (10)
- Find owner/operations/marketing lead per account.
- Add one concrete personalization line from site/LinkedIn activity.

### Day 4: Trigger validation (10)
- Add a "why now" trigger:
  - Recent hiring
  - New service launch
  - Active posting but weak conversion path
  - Growth signals (new team page, new offer, expansion)

### Day 5: Quality gate + finalize (5)
- Remove low-fit duplicates.
- Ensure all 50 records have fit score and personalization note.
- Final output split:
  - Batch 1: top 20 (score 5)
  - Batch 2: next 20 (score 4)
  - Batch 3: remaining 10 (score 3-4)

- One CTA per touch: 15-minute verkenningsgesprek

## 4. Execution Checklist (first 2 weeks)
- Finalize target list with 50 records and scoring
- Launch batch 1 (20 leads)
- Track replies by sentiment (`positive`, `neutral`, `negative`, `no_response`)
- Launch batch 2 only after first 20 reply review
- Iterate subject line + CTA based on first 10 responses

## 5. KPI Targets (Month 1)
- 50 qualified targets completed
- >=20% reply rate on Tier A
- >=10% positive reply rate on Tier A
- >=3 meetings booked
- >=1 partnership intro
